Questions

Before you book

01Where is the studio?
In Taubaté, São Paulo state, about 130 km from São Paulo city on the highway to Rio de Janeiro. The exact address comes with your booking.
02When in my trip should I get tattooed?
Near the end. Sun, sea and pools slow the healing, so plan beach days before the session.
03Can I get one of the flash designs?
Yes. Name the design in your request. Flash days with special prices are announced on Instagram.
04Do you sell prints and drawings?
Yes, to order. Choose “A print or drawing” in the form and say which one.
